# Closed-pilot scope and evidence

State: updated 17 October. MVP scope, technical/moderation defaults, poster invitations, ordinary signup and taxonomy baseline adopted. Provider/legal/runtime activation gates remain; DECISIONS.md wins.

## Product thesis

Shutaf brings apartment/room and roommate discovery into one native hub so students do not have to coordinate a fragmented search across social groups and stories. Beer Sheva is the initial acquisition/default area, not a hard publication boundary (A5). The pilot proves useful interactions, not the mere existence of map pins.

## Proposed proof journeys

1. **Available room:** an actual lister/resident publishes two rooms; a guest sees safe public information; a resident saves/inquires; owner accepts; both chat and authorized resident sees address; owner changes a room's availability without losing the other.
2. **Find people then a place:** resident completes profile; sees a plausible compatibility explanation; introduction is accepted; both consent to form a group; they share an active listing inside their group chat and continue apartment hunting. Creating the group never closes a listing.
3. **Trust and recovery:** stranger cannot obtain exact address or private profile fields; block takes effect through direct APIs and groups; report reaches reviewer; invalid upload/provider failure leaves a recoverable state; account exit follows approved data policy.
4. **Bring users back:** offline recipient receives an enabled message/inquiry notification, follows an authorized deep link, resumes the correct conversation; disable stops future sends.

Live demonstrations may use clearly marked synthetic accounts/data. Pilot evidence needs actual consenting people and confirmed active inventory. Separate those cohorts in any investor presentation.

## Completion gates — proposed, measurable

- All included journeys pass using ordinary clients in two or more browser contexts, and persist after reload.
- Verify Hebrew RTL and English LTR, phone and desktop, including keyboard/address fallback/error recovery.
- Versioned backend target, migrations and source SHA identified; no assertion that Preview means isolated database.
- No pending high-severity public/private data boundary or owner/outsider authorization failure.
- All created QA accounts/uploads/data cleaned and counts recorded; actual pilot users never used as disposable fixtures.
- A reviewer can handle a report; operator knows how to pause enrollment/sending and investigate failures.
- Dashboard/report distinguishes registrations from completed profiles, responsive inventory, introductions, accepted inquiries and replies.
- Owner-approved open-policy gates in BUILD_BLUEPRINT resolved for included features. Legal/provider-dependent obligations explicitly assigned; closed pilot is not an exemption from privacy duties.
- Demo script can be repeated from a known version; known limitations stated rather than hidden.

Recruiting numbers/deadline remain unset; operating budget is free-only Q32. Suggested experiment: a small manually recruited cohort and enough confirmed rooms for representative searches, not a funding criterion. Supply outreach is deferred Q3 and is not an immediate implementation blocker.

## Measurement interpretation

This is an episodic product. A person who finds a place may correctly stop searching. Measure return among people still searching, time to useful reply and declared outcome; do not optimize endless swiping or treat every successful departure as churn. No claim that completing a demo proves product-market fit or qualifies for a specific fund.
